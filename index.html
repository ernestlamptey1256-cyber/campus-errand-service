How it works

Three steps from message to doorstep.

1

Message us

Tell us what you need and where to deliver it, on WhatsApp or by phone call.

2

Get your fee and runner

We confirm your fee, the estimated time and your runner’s name before we start.

3

Receive and pay

Get your order with updates along the way, then pay by mobile money.

Built on trust

You are handing us your money and belongings, so we make every step visible.

Identified runners

Every runner is screened, trained, and wears an ID. You get their name or code before they start.

Live updates

We message you at each stage, such as item bought and on the way.

Receipts for everything

Every purchase comes with a receipt, and you get exact change.

Fee quoted first

No hidden charges. The price you agree is the price you pay.

What if something goes wrong?

Tell us within 30 minutes for food and groceries, or within 24 hours for other items, with a photo if you can. We investigate and replace or refund within our limits (up to GHc 150 per order during the pilot). Late orders caused by us earn a refund of part or all of the service fee.

How do I pay?

Pay the item cost by mobile money before we buy, and the service fee on delivery or by mobile money. We never ask you to pay anyone else.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free of charge before the runner starts. After that, you pay for items already bought and a reduced fee.

What will you not do?

We do not run illegal errands, write or complete academic work, or go to places that are unsafe for our runners.
